Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a carefully art-directed, production-quality mobile healthcare UI concept: TWO complete app screens side by side, Home and Doctor profile, for CareMatch. This is a new design from the content below. It should look like actual professional mobile software ready for implementation, with the restraint of a mature editorial healthcare brand and the precision of a well-built native app. No device mockups. No presentation headline. Straight-on flat screen exports on a neutral #E9EEED background with narrow gap and quiet square-ish corners. Each frame is approximately 390x844 proportions. Make every text readable and the full bottom navigation/footer visible.

DESIGN LANGUAGE: 90% warm-neutral white; dark charcoal-teal #163238 text; sophisticated teal #087F8C spent sparingly on actions and selection. Secondary grey #60716F. 1px warm grey rules. Restrained pale teal #EFF7F5 only where it defines an interactive region. Crisp Manrope-style headings, Inter-style body. Natural mobile sizes: screen title around 26px, section headings 18px, body 14-15px, metadata 12px. Use moderate weights, never everything bold. 24px side margins, consistent alignment and an 8px spacing rhythm. Corners 10-12px, buttons 12px corners. Distinguish surfaces through spacing and thin borders, almost no shadows. Real human medical headshots, natural daylight, authentic professional clothing, cropped cleanly; not cartoons or plastic CGI. All icons one consistent simple thin-line family. Fine proportions and detailed optical alignment matter.

DO NOT use oversized hero copy, large avatars, glowing gradients, aurora blobs, sparkles, glassmorphism, floating bubbly icon clusters, saturated marketing banners, excessive pills, repeated elevated cards, decorative plants, 3D icons, purple or rainbow accents, swooshes, dramatic shadows, fake phone hardware, unnecessary badges, giant empty gaps. Quiet, useful, meticulously typeset rather than decorative. Restrained professional contrast and real content make the design interesting.
