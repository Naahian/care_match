Suggested type scale: 32/38 headings, 16/24 body, 16/20 buttons, 12/16 supporting labels. Use 24px horizontal margins, 20px card corners, 56px primary buttons, generous whitespace. Keep consistent header, content zones, progress and bottom actions across the sequence. Preserve CareMatch brand mark/name; original vector asset to be obtained from project or user if available. ## 1. Describe your symptoms

Headline: Describe your symptoms
Supporting copy: Tell us how you’re feeling. Our AI helps match you with a nearby specialist.
Focal visual: softly tinted AI matching panel, symptom message and specialist-match card, clear visual progression from symptom to doctor. Sample message: “I have a sore throat and a mild fever.” Label any recommendation data as illustrative in handoff notes. Avoid invented ratings or real availability claims.
Footer: first of four progress positions; Next button with arrow.

## 2. Consult via chat or video

Headline: Care that fits your day
Supporting copy: Chat with a doctor, start a video consultation, or book an in-person visit.
Focal visual: calm consultation card, video placeholder/illustration and compact chat exchange. Three clearly differentiated modality chips: Chat, Video, In person. Use sample content and no claim that the preview is a live call.
Footer: second progress position; Next.

## 3. Your health data stays private

Design theme: privacy and records control. Focal visual: a simple shield/lock composition with quiet rounded information cards rather than four competing badges.
Source claims pending verification: end-to-end encryption, information never leaves phone, stored only on device, HIPAA compliance, no sharing without consent. Do not publish or present these as verified product properties.
Provisional headline: Your care, in one place
Provisional supporting copy: Keep track of your reports and prescriptions as you move through your care journey.
Provisional cards: Reports; Prescriptions. This copy uses confirmed product capabilities while preserving the third step's care-records context; final privacy copy requires product confirmation. Record this copy change explicitly in delivery.
Footer: third progress position; Next.

## 4. Better care starts here

Headline: Better care starts here
Supporting copy: Find a nearby specialist, consult your way, and keep your care organised.
Focal visual: a compact, balanced patient-to-doctor matching illustration or editable UI arrangement, with gentle mint atmosphere. Four supporting feature tiles: Find a doctor, Consult, Reports, Prescriptions. In-person booking is covered in screen 2.
Footer: Get started primary action; Already have an account? Log in secondary action; fourth progress position. Maintain clear separation and tap targets.
